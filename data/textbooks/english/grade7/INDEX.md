# english 教材结构说明

这本教材共有 5 章：
第1章：第1章 Starter Unit，包含 2 个知识点：Section A: How do you greet people?、Section B: How do we get to know each other?
第2章：第2章 Unit 1 You and Me，包含 2 个知识点：Section A: How do we make new friends?、Section B: What do we need to know about a new friend?
第3章：第3章 Unit 2 We're Family!，包含 2 个知识点：Section A: What is your family like?、Section B: What do you like about your family?
第4章：第4章 Unit 3 Welcome!，包含 2 个知识点：Section A: Where do you put your things?、Section B: What is fun on a farm?
第5章：第5章 Unit 4 My School，包含 2 个知识点：Section A: What is your school like?、Section B: What do you like about your school?
